# ArkiPlan – building plan sets on Android

**Developed by Darvin.**

ArkiPlan draws a residential or commercial building from the room sizes you type in and generates a full plan set in the format of a Philippine building-permit set (A3 sheets, title block, Office of the Building Official box).

## How it works

1. **New Project** asks, one step at a time: project name, building type and number of storeys → owner name and address → project location (province / municipality) → architect / engineer / drawn by → wall thickness and heights (exterior & interior wall, floor to top of beam, ceiling, door/window head, NGL to FFL) → roof type, slope, overhang and sheet size.
2. **Plan tab** (doors D1–D7; windows W1 sliding, W2 wide sliding, W3 single casement, W4 T&B awning, W5 fixed, W6 storefront, W7 jalousie, W8 T&B jalousie, W9 double casement, W10 awning; doors keep at least 100 mm and windows 50 mm from any wall face, column or other opening – adjustable in ⚙) – tap **+ Room**, pick the space type (living, bedroom, T&B, kitchen, porch, carport, store…), type its width and length in metres and say which existing room it sits next to (right / left / below / above, aligned top-left / centered / bottom-right). Sizes are center-to-center of walls (grid to grid).
   - Walls are built automatically (exterior vs interior thickness, shared walls merged, open areas such as porch/carport get no wall).
   - Grid lines are generated from the room edges: **A, B, C… on the vertical lines**, **1, 2, 3… on the horizontal lines**, with mm dimension strings.
   - Columns are placed where walls meet on grid intersections and sized from spans, storeys and soil bearing (C1, C2… / F1, F2…). **These are preliminary – a licensed civil engineer must verify them.**
   - **Door / Window** – pick a template (D1 main door, D2 bedroom, D3 toilet, D4 double, D5 sliding glass, D6 roll-up, D7 exit door – steel fire exit door that swings out, marked EXIT with an arrow, and an EXIT sign with battery back-up is added inside it on the lighting layout; W1/W2 sliding, W3 casement, W4 awning for T&B, W5 fixed, W6 storefront) and tap a wall. Tap a door/window to change its size, sill, position, swing or hinge. Window heads line up with the door height by default (a 1200 mm high window at 2100 door height gets a 900 mm sill; change the height and the sill follows); choose **Own sill height** under Window head to set a different sill.
   - While placing a door/window, drag along the wall: blue **temporary dimensions** show the clear distance to the nearest column, wall face or opening on each side, it **snaps to center** (green "EQ · CENTERED") and to 50 mm steps, and turns red if it does not fit. Release to place – the temporary dimensions disappear. Tap a placed door/window and drag it to move it, or type exact "From left / From right" distances under **Edit**.
   - Tap a room to select it and drag it to move it (it snaps to other room edges). Drag one of the four **blue wall handles** to resize: rooms sharing that wall adjust with it, areas update live, doors and windows stay in place. While dragging (or after tapping a handle) a blue **Width / Length box** appears next to the handle: type the exact size in mm and press Enter (or ✓) and the wall moves to that dimension. Undo/redo at the top.
   - Toilets, kitchens, bedrooms, living, dining, laundry, carport and stairs get fixtures/furniture automatically. **⟳ Furniture** in the room panel turns the layout to the next wall. **Auto furniture: on/off** hides the automatic furniture of that room (plumbing fixtures stay).
   - Stairs get a **railing (H=900)** on each open side of the flight, or a wall **handrail** when both sides have walls; a section cut along the flight shows the steps, waist slab, railing and riser/tread count, with the stair well left open in the floor above.
   - **🔓 Lock size** (room panel) locks a room: it cannot be dragged, resized, rotated or retyped, its handles disappear, and neighbours cannot move the walls they share with it. A padlock marks locked rooms; tap **🔒 Size locked** to unlock.
   - **🛋 Open room** (room panel, or 🛋 Furniture on the toolbar) zooms into that room and greys out the rest. Inside, **🛋 Add furniture** places pieces only in that room, and furniture can never be dragged or rotated through its walls. Its doors and windows can be tapped, dragged and edited there too, and **+ Door** / **+ Window** add new ones. **← Back to plan** / **Done** returns to the whole plan.
   - **🔓 Lock** in the panel of a door, window or furniture piece locks it: it cannot be dragged (plan, room view or elevations), changed, flipped or deleted until you tap **🔒 Locked** again. Locked pieces show a small padlock.
   - **🛋 Furniture** adds your own pieces: TV & console, sofas, armchair, coffee table, cabinet, dining sets, refrigerator, kitchen sink, stove, counter, beds, wardrobe, desk, dresser, washing machine, bathtub, water closet, lavatory and car (template blocks from AGI-TEMPLATES where available). Tap in a room: the back goes against the nearest wall. A TV, desk or kitchen counter gets a convenience outlet behind it on the power layout, a refrigerator a REF outlet, and a washing machine or stove its own circuit in the schedule of loads. In Edit, tap a piece to select it, drag to move it, and use **Rotate 90°**, **Duplicate**, **Delete** or type its size/position. Furniture moves with its room when you drag the room.
   - **Every fixture and piece is editable**: inside an opened room (🛋 Open room) tap its toilet, lavatory, shower, floor drain, sink, stove, counter, washer, laundry tub, bed, sofa or table – the room's automatic pieces become your own, so you can move, rotate, duplicate, delete and **stretch** them (drag the 4 blue handles on the selected piece, the size shows live; or type width / depth). The plumbing lines, power outlets and toilet details follow the moved pieces. Kitchens big enough get an **L-shaped counter** (back wall + a leg on a side without a door); any straight counter can be turned into an L (**Make L-shape**, **Mirror L**, counter depth), and the L counter is in the furniture list too.
   - **Moving rooms**: long-press a room (about half a second) and drag – the whole room moves and the rooms next to it keep their size; drag a blue handle to resize. Furniture is moved / stretched only inside the opened room.
   - **Floor / Walls / Ceiling** (room panel, or the toolbar inside 🛋 Open room): **Floor** – materials (+ for more than one) and the floor level below FFL (e.g. T&B −50 mm: shown on the plan, sections, schedule, 3D); **Walls** – materials and tile height for each side (N / E / S / W) and the wall thickness; **Ceiling** – flat ceiling or none (exposed), its height and materials (sections, schedule, T&B details, 3D › Ceilings).
   - **👁 Inside – all walls** (in Open room): the room's four walls seen from inside with doors, windows, fixtures, counters, tiles, finishes and ceiling height; **🏠 3D inside** stands you in the room at eye height in the 3D tab – drag to look around every side, pinch to step back.
   - **Pictures** (Plan tab › ▦ Lot / Site): upload a vicinity map, a location map (town / province) and your own perspective picture; they go on A-1 (with a location map A-1 has 5 views). Details › Perspective (drawn by the app) keeps the generated one.
   - **Scale of the drawings** (Sheets tab › 📐 Scale): for the sheet on screen pick Auto (largest that fits) or 1:20 … 1:500 for each drawing; drawings that belong together (floor plans, lighting & power layouts) keep one scale. The title under each drawing shows its scale.
   - **Dimensions**: the three strings around the plans (doors & windows, column to column, overall) are evenly spaced; ⚙ settings › Dimensions turns the doors & windows string off.
   - **✋ View / ✎ Edit**: the editor opens in View mode – drag to pan, pinch to zoom, taps change nothing. Switch to Edit to select and change rooms, doors and windows. The tools (+ Room, Door, Window, Walls, Columns) work in either mode.
   - **Finishes** (room panel): floor, wall, wall tile height and ceiling finish – pick a common finish or type your own. They appear in the **Schedule of Finishes** sheet, in the sections (floor layer, wall tile hatch, FLR/WALL/CLG notes), as the floor tile pattern in the plan and in the toilet details. The exterior wall finish (⚙ settings) is noted on the elevations.
   - Walls and columns are protected: they can only be selected while their tool is switched on, so normal tapping never deletes them.
   - **✂ Walls** tool: tap a wall → only the piece between the nearest columns / wall corners is selected → **Delete this piece** (it becomes a dashed open boundary – open-plan kitchen/dining, open lanai side; doors/windows on it are hidden). Tap a dashed line → **Restore wall**. **Done** turns the tool off.
   - **+ Add wall** (in the Walls tool banner): drag to draw a straight partition wall – it snaps to walls, grids and room edges and shows its length live; afterwards type the exact length, choose 100/150/200 mm thickness or delete it. Doors and windows can be placed on drawn walls. (Drawn walls are partitions; use **+ Room** for spaces that need their own name and area.)
   - **Grid lines** (A, B, C… / 1, 2, 3…) are drawn only on lines that carry columns: delete all columns on a line and that grid disappears, and the rest are re-lettered / re-numbered.
   - **■ Columns** tool: tap a column → choose its **Shape** (square, rectangular with width × depth, or circular with a diameter; **Make all columns …** applies it to every column), resize it or **Delete column** (neighbouring columns/footings re-size for the longer span); **+ Add column** in the banner adds one, snapping to grid intersections. Deleted columns can be restored under **Grids & Columns**.
   - **⌂ Roof** tool (like Revit): the roofs are drawn over the plan with their ridges / hips and a slope arrow. Tap a roof → drag its blue handles to move that eave (the roof follows; edges snap to the walls), drag the roof itself to move it, or type its width / length. In the panel: **Roof type** (any design, or the project setting), **Sits on** (which floor's walls), slope, overhang, ridge direction (**Turn ridge**), **Flip slope** for shed roofs, **Fit to walls**, **Duplicate**, **Delete roof**. **+ Add roof**: drag a rectangle, or tap a room to cover it – e.g. a lean-to over a porch or carport, a flat R.C. deck for a terrace, or a different roof over a lower wing. **Automatic** goes back to the single roof that follows the top floor. All roofs appear in the roof plan, roof framing plan, elevations, sections, perspective and site plan.
   - **🏗 Levels** (Revit-like structural levels): pick NGL (footings), Ground floor level (tie beams), each upper floor's beam level and floor level, Roof beam level and Roof level at the top. Each shows the floor plan faded with the columns and beams on top: tap a beam to change its width / depth (or the same size for all on that level) or delete it; **+ Beam / + Tie beam / + Roof beam** drags a new one from column to column; **Reset beams** goes back to the automatic ones. Marks follow the sizes (TB-1, TB-2, B-1, RB-1 …) and the foundation plan (tie beams), beam plans, SCHEDULE OF BEAMS and beam details use them.
   - **Level heights** (Levels › ↕ Height): NGL below FFL; for any floor / beam level its elevation (moving it changes the storey under it, the levels above move too) and the height to the next level; the roof beam level the same. Each floor keeps its own height (⚙ settings › Floor to top of beam is the default). Sections, elevations, stairs, level marks, plumbing isometrics and 3D follow.
   - **Phone / Tablet layout** (button on the home screen, ▣ in the editor bar): Auto (tablet when the screen is 900 px wide or more), Phone, or Tablet – tabs in a rail on the left, the toolbar wraps, the edit panel on the right (under the drawing when the tablet is upright).
   - **Tools show Done / Cancel on top**: while a tool is on (door / window placing, walls, columns, furniture), the toolbar shows ✓ Done, ✕ Cancel (undoes everything done with that tool) and what to do – no floating bubbles over the drawing.
   - **3D tab**: the whole building in a SketchUp-style 3-D model: solid walls with their thickness cut around the openings, window frames with glass, door leaves, gable walls up to the roof, slabs, posts and beams on open sides, stairs, the furniture & fixtures as blocks, every roof, black edges, sun shadows and a sky. One finger turns it around, two fingers pinch to zoom and move to pan (mouse: drag / wheel / right-drag). Buttons: **3D, Front, Rear, Left, Right, Top**; ⛶ resets the view. **Whole house** or **Cut at <floor>** takes the roof (and the floors above) off to look inside. Uses the bundled three.js (MIT, `www/lib`), works offline.
3. **Elevation tab** – front / rear / left / right, generated live with roof, posts, openings, level marks (NGL, FFL, DOORS & WINDOW HT., TOP OF BEAM, TOP OF ROOF) and grid bubbles. Switch to **✎ Edit** here (or in the Section tab) and tap a door or window: drag it sideways to move it along its wall (same clearances and snapping as in the plan), drag a window up/down to change its sill, or edit it in the panel below.
4. **Section tab** – Section A and B (by default they cut through the toilet), with slabs, cut walls, footings, ceiling lines, the roof truss and the doors and windows on the walls seen beyond the cut (as seen from inside). You can type the cut position.
5. **Details tab** – roof plan, doors & windows schedule, foundation plan, beam plans, roof framing plan (steel trusses & purlins), truss detail, column & footing schedule, footing, wall footing, column and beam details, room schedule, and for each toilet an enlarged plan plus 4 interior wall elevations (tiles, WC, lavatory, shower).
6. **Electrical & plumbing** are generated automatically too (Details tab, and E / P sheets):
   - **E** – as few sheets as the drawings allow: a small project gets everything on ONE sheet (lighting + power layouts at up to 1:125 with the schedule of loads, riser diagram, notes and legend); a 2-storey house gets TWO sheets (each floor's lighting + power layouts at the same scale, down to 1:150; schedule of loads + riser on the first, notes + legend on the second); bigger projects get one sheet per floor plus the schedules. Lighting layout (LED lights, exhaust fans, switches at the door latch side, wiring and home runs), power layout (convenience outlets, aircon, range, refrigerator, water heater and washer outlets, panel board LP and kWh meter), schedule of loads with circuit breakers and wire sizes, load computation and main breaker, single line diagram, legend and PEC notes.
   - **P** – sanitary layout (soil, waste and vent lines, cleanouts, floor drains, stacks, grease trap and septic tank), storm drainage (downspouts, catch basins, outfall), waterline layout (water meter, gate valve, header, risers, hose bibb), then for every floor an **isometric view of the waterline layout** and **of the sewer line layout** (the floor plan laid flat in isometric in light grey, with the pipes drawn bold on top, sizes written along each run, risers, vents and stacks standing up, septic tank and storm lines on the ground floor), isometric riser diagrams, septic tank / catch basin / floor drain / cleanout details, legend and plumbing notes.
   - Wire, breaker and pipe sizes are preliminary: a Professional Electrical Engineer and a Master Plumber must check and sign them.
7. **Units** – millimeters by default; centimeters, meters, inches or feet-inches can be chosen in the wizard or ⚙ settings. Every size you type and every dimension, schedule and area (sq.m. / sq.ft.) follows the chosen unit.
8. **Lot / Site** (Plan tab toolbar) – type or paste the **technical description**, one course per line (e.g. `1-2  N 45°30' E  20.00`; a line with TIE / BLLM / BBM is the tie line). The lot area and error of closure are computed as you type. Pick which lot line faces the road, its name and width, and the front setback / shift / turn of the building. **Open Google Maps** opens the project location; take a screenshot and **Import map image** for the vicinity map.
   Sheet **A-1** then shows the **perspective** (3-D view generated from the model), the **vicinity map**, the **lot plan** (bearings, distances, corner numbers, tie line, technical description table, lot area) and the **site development plan** (building on the lot, setbacks to every lot line, road, area data with % lot occupancy). The sheet index moves to A-2. The perspective has dark outlines with the eave, hip and ridge lines drawn over the roof. The perspective is also in the Details tab.
9. **Sheets tab** – the finished plan set (A architectural, S structural, E electrical, P plumbing). Sheet A-2 is the **Sheet Index** (sheet no., contents, page x of y). Every view title shows its view number over its sheet number, and the plans carry cross-reference tags: section tags (A / A-5), exterior elevation tags (1–4 / A-4), T&B enlarged-plan callouts and the typical footing callout on the foundation plan. Export:
   - **PDF (all)** – vector PDF, one page per sheet, ready to print.
   - **DXF (CAD)** – all views at 1:1 in millimetres with layers (WALLS, GRID, DIMS, DOORS, WINDOWS, COLUMNS, ROOF…) to continue in AutoCAD.
   - **PNG** – the current sheet as an image.
   - **Backup** – the project file (.json); use **Import** on the home screen to restore it.

10. **Roofs that follow the walls** – an L, T or U shaped floor gets a roof that follows its outline: a true hip roof with hips and valleys, or one gable per wing, running on into the main roof. The roof plan, roof framing (trusses per wing), gutters, elevations, sections and 3D all follow. **Roof › ✎ Sketch roof** draws a roof like Revit's roof by footprint: tap the corners along the walls (snaps to the walls, squared lines), then tap the first point or **✓ Close outline**. Blue handles move one edge of an outline roof at a time.
11. **Dimensions** – inside: doors & windows (optional), then column to column; outside: overhang | overall (first to last grid) | overhang. ⚙ Settings › **grid bubbles** on / off.
12. **Walls on levels** – Plan › Levels › a floor level or the **Roof beam level** › **+ Wall**. Walls on the roof beams (parapet / fire wall) have their own height and show on the roof plan, elevations, sections and in 3D. A drawn wall can be lower than the storey (Height).
13. **Edit walls in elevations & sections** – ✎ Edit, then tap a wall: thickness, height (drawn walls), storey height (room walls), exterior finish, delete (drawn walls).
14. **Half truss detail (HT-1)** – shed and double shed roofs get a half (mono-pitch) truss detail on S-2; the framing plan marks them HT-1.
15. **Floor slab thickness per room** – open a room › Floor › Floor slab thickness (uneven floors); the sections and 3D follow.

16. **Drawn walls** – Walls › + Add wall first asks the wall type: material (CHB, RC, brick, drywall, fiber cement, wood, glass block), thickness, height and the finish on each side; tap a drawn wall to change them. A selected drawn wall can be dragged (one direction at a time, snaps to wall lines & grids) and shows temporary dimensions to the walls around it – tap a number to type the distance.
17. **Roof lines** – ✎ Sketch roof first asks the roof type, slope, offset (overhang past the line) and height offset (raise / lower the roof from the top of the beam); every roof has the height offset in its panel.
18. **Trusses** – Roof › ⟂ Trusses: place trusses by hand (drag across the roof wall to wall, a half truss starts at its high end), tap one to change its type, copy it or delete it. ⚙ Truss types: mark, full / half, style (Pratt, Howe, Warren, Fink, King post, Queen post), material (steel C-channel, steel tubular, wood), member sizes and depth. Each type used gets its own detail on S-2; a roof can switch its automatic trusses off or give them a type. Trusses can run at any angle (squared within 6° of level / plumb); a selected truss can be dragged to move it, or by an end to stretch it. **📐 View / edit detail** opens the truss drawing: drag any joint, + Member (joint to joint), split or delete members and joints, or go back to the style – the change applies to every truss of that type and to its detail on S-2.
20. **Lights & power** – Plan › ⚡ Lights & Power (or Details › Electrical – lighting / power layout): pick a light, exhaust fan, switch, exit sign or outlet (convenience, weatherproof, aircon, range, refrigerator, water heater, washer) and + Add, then tap where it goes. Tap any symbol to drag it, change it (aircon HP, switch gang, WP) or delete it; Back to the automatic place / Reset this layout undo your changes. The circuits, schedule of loads, riser and E sheets follow. Light types: recessed downlight, pin light, surface ceiling light, pendant, T8 tube, LED panel, track, wall light, chandelier, emergency light (own symbol, load and legend line); outlet types: duplex, weatherproof, GFCI, with USB, floor outlet. New / moved lights snap to the room's centre (and quarter lines, and in line with other lights); outlets and switches snap onto the nearest wall. Drag the LP (panel board) to any wall – the kWh meter goes outside it and every home run follows; 🗑 Delete layout clears a floor's lights (or outlets). Lights can go outside too (eaves, porch ceiling) and line up with a room's centre. Tap any light or outlet › Circuit: Automatic, any existing circuit (e.g. put the outside light on circuit 1), or a new circuit – it leaves its old circuit; tick "Also every light of the room" to move the whole room. The circuit list beside + Add picks the circuit of new ones. The first time you pick a circuit the others are kept as they are, so the numbers don't shift; the room's lights are wired per circuit. The schedule of loads, riser and home runs follow. **🔗 Connect to…** (in a light's / outlet's panel): tap the light, outlet, switch or LP it is wired from – the wire is drawn from that one and it joins its circuit (connected to LP = its own home run); ✂ Disconnect goes back to the automatic wiring. **⚡ Circuits** (lighting / power layout): + Lighting circuit, + Power circuit (new ones are picked for + Add), rename a circuit, change its type (lighting / convenience / appliance), remove the ones you made, and Renumber (lighting first, then power, then appliances). Lights outside the house are wired one to the next on their circuit. Tap the LP › **📋 Open panel board**: main breaker, feeder, total load, and every circuit with its breaker, wire, load and what is on it (and where); **Show** highlights that circuit's lights / outlets on the layout.
19. **Snap** – 🧲 Snap on every tool that adds or moves something: wall lines, wall faces & ends, grids, columns, middle of walls, step size. Every edit panel has a ✓ OK button (Enter does the same).

The doors & windows schedule lists only the doors and windows drawn on the plans.

Projects are saved automatically on the device.

## Try it now (no install)

```bash
node dev-server.js
```

Then open http://localhost:5173 in a browser. To try it on your phone, connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi and open `http://<your-PC-IP>:5173`.

## Build the Android APK

The app is a web app wrapped with [Capacitor](https://capacitorjs.com/). You need **Node.js** (installed) and **Android Studio** (includes the Android SDK and Java).

> Build from a local folder (for example `C:\dev\arkiplan`), not from Google Drive – `node_modules` has thousands of small files and Drive sync makes builds very slow.

```bash
npm install
npx cap add android
npx cap sync android
npx cap open android
```

In Android Studio: **Build → Build Bundle(s) / APK(s) → Build APK(s)**. The APK is written to `android/app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`; copy it to your phone and install it (allow "install unknown apps").

After changing anything in `www/`, run `npx cap sync android` again before rebuilding.

### Without Android Studio (GitHub)

Push this folder to a GitHub repository. The workflow in `.github/workflows/android.yml` builds the debug APK on every push; download it from the run's **Artifacts** section.

| Path | What it does |
|---|---|
| `www/index.html`, `www/css/app.css` | screens: home, project wizard, editor |
| `www/js/app.js` | user interface, touch pan/zoom, dialogs, exports |

| `www/js/drawing.js` | vector engine → SVG (screen), DXF (AutoCAD), PDF (print) |

## Limits of this first version

- Rooms are rectangles; walls follow room edges (no angled or curved walls).
- Roof gutters (150 mm G.I. with 75 mm uPVC downspouts) are drawn along every eave on the roof plan, elevations and sections, with a gutter detail on the structural details sheet.
- By default one roof covers the top floor and follows its walls; with the ⌂ Roof tool you can reshape it and add more roofs (outlines are rectangles). Roof designs: hip, gable, dutch gable (hip-and-gable), steep hip (Bahay Kubo / Bahay na Bato style), shed / mono-slope, split shed with clerestory windows, double shed (high and low mono-slopes both draining to the front, clerestory band between), butterfly / V-roof, modern box (roof hidden behind parapet) and flat R.C. roof deck.
- Structural sizes are rule-of-thumb estimates for preliminary design only.
