ArkiPlan – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · App: ArkiPlan (com.arkiplan.app) · Developer: Darvin

ArkiPlan is an app for drawing house plans and building-permit plan sets. This policy explains what information the app uses and how. In short: your projects stay on your device. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your projects (room sizes, lot descriptions, owner and project names you type, settings, material prices) and any pictures you add (vicinity map, location map, perspective, sketch photos) are saved only in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to any server operated by us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

2. Files you export or share

When you download a PDF, DXF, PNG or a project backup, the file is saved where you choose or handed to the Android share sheet. Where it goes after that (Files, Google Drive, e-mail, messaging apps) is decided by you and is covered by those services' own privacy policies.

3. Subscriptions and payments

ArkiPlan Pro is a monthly subscription sold through Google Play. Payments, billing details and subscription management are handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only asks Google Play whether your account has an active ArkiPlan Pro subscription; we never see or store your payment card or billing information. The free-trial start date is stored on your device.

4. Maps and links

The "Open Google Maps" button opens Google Maps (app or website) at the project location you typed, so you can take screenshots for the vicinity map. Google Maps is governed by Google's privacy policy.

5. Permissions

ArkiPlan uses internet access (to check your Google Play subscription and to open links) and lets you pick pictures and save files through Android's own pickers. It does not use your location, contacts, camera in the background, microphone or advertising ID.

6. Analytics and advertising

ArkiPlan contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

7. Children

ArkiPlan is a professional design tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
